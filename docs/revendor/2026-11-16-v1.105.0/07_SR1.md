Written after this bundle was recorded, and the bundle stays as it was. This is item `SR-MM` of

## The re-cut tag

The local LibKa0s tag `v1.105.0` was re-cut at `2cd727d5` to carry `SR-L1` to `SR-L4`. It is not on
origin, which is expected. Both payloads were copied whole from it (`git archive v1.105.0 LibKa0s
testkit tools`) into `libs/LibKa0s/` and `tests/_kit/`. The tag's `tools/prune-icons.lua` then ran
against the existing `LibKa0s icons:` declaration, which already names `settings`. There is no
`LibKa0s sounds:` line, so no sound files are kept. The CLAUDE.md provenance line still names v1.105.0.

## What the re-copy changed

- **Fifteen library files:** Charts, Core, DebugLog, DebugLogWindow, IconBrowser, OptionsSkinPicker,
  SimPanel, SkinDefs, SkinDefsOthers, Tooltip, WidgetsDragHandle, WidgetsDragHandleTip,
  WidgetsDropdown, WidgetsHeader and WidgetsReorder. Together they account for 306 insertions and
  124 deletions. No LibStub minor moved from the first cut.
- **No change** to the kit (`tests/_kit/` is identical to `40a7297`'s), the icon set or the sounds.
- **One case re-pinned** (`SR-L4`). `test_window_header.lua`'s "Column headers have their own color
  and background" had passed only because Ka0s Default's `list.header.bg` used to be white .04, and
  its blue channel happened to match the swatch. The band is now black .35, so the case turns the
  companion (`columnHeader.bgColorFromSkin`) off and asserts the player's swatch. The comment in
  `defaults/Profile.lua` now says "a dark band".

## Title-bar pass, per window

The library surfaces were used as they are: `LibKa0s-Tooltip-1.0`'s `AttachTitleTip` and `CloseTip`,
Core's `MakeCloseButton` with the owner, and the window's cached skin looks. `W.HeaderButtons` was
**not** adopted. The strip has its own art ladder, reveal and per-window color modes, so attaching tips
and changing one token was the smaller change.

- **Meter window** (`modules/HeaderControls.lua`). It has seven controls: close, minimize, lock,
  settings, segment, reset and export.
  - **Tooltips.** Every control gets `AttachTitleTip` with owner `MultiMeters`. The tooltip sits
    above the control, flips below when there is no room, and uses `gtip.title`. The words are read
    on every hover:
    - close: "Close" (`Tooltip.CloseTip`)
    - minimize: "Minimize window" or "Expand window"
    - lock: "Lock window" or "Unlock window"
    - settings: "Open settings"
    - segment: "Choose a segment"
    - reset: "Reset meter data"
    - export: "Export a segment to CSV or to chat" (this key was already declared but unused)
  - **Colors.** These apply under the shipped `skin` modes. At rest every control paints
    `icon.tint`. Under the pointer the six controls paint `text.link`, which was `btn.hover` before.
    The close keeps `close.rest`, which follows `icon.tint` in every skin since `SR-L3`, and paints
    `close.hot` under the pointer. The strip had no glow to keep: its existing hover feedback is the
    reveal's alpha, and that is unchanged. Lock and minimize show their state with the glyph, not
    with a color, so they take the same rest and hover colors as the others. The `class` and `custom`
    modes are unchanged.
  - **Host copy.** `NS.SKIN_FALLBACK` gives the degraded install `text.link` in place of `btn.hover`.
    `controlHover` now resolves `text.link` (`core/SkinSetup.lua`).
- **Export modal** (`modules/Export_Modal.lua`). Its only control is the library close, built by
  `NS.MakeCloseButton` with the owner. The re-copy gives it "Close" above the button in
  `gtip.title`, `icon.tint` at rest and `close.hot` under the pointer. No code changed. A case
  now pins this behavior.
- The copy window, the debug console, the perf panel, the sim panel and the skin picker belong to the
  library, and their tests live in the library's suite.

**Red first.** The new suite is `tests/test_titlebar.lua`, with four cases, all of them run under
both Ka0s Default and Obsidian:

- Every meter-window control shows a non-empty tooltip in `gtip.title` and hides it on leave.
- The two state controls change their words with their state.
- Rest and hover colors resolve to `icon.tint`, `text.link` and `close.hot`.
- The Export modal's close is checked the same way.

The first three cases were red before the change. The fourth was green from the re-copy alone.
Cases that pinned the old gold hover were re-pinned to `text.link`: three in
`test_headercontrols.lua` and `test_skin_window.lua`'s `assertFollows`.
